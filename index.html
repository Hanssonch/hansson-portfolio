About

Hansson Cornelius Huang is a Finance undergraduate at BINUS University with a focus on equity research, corporate valuation, and financial modeling. His work spans fundamental analysis of Indonesian listed companies, segment-driven discounted cash flow models, trading comparables, and pro-forma modeling for merger and acquisition cases.

Alongside his studies, he serves as an equity research analyst at the Binus Finance Club and has completed a financial analyst internship covering reconciliations, month-end closing, and variance analysis. He aspires to a career in high finance, with an emphasis on institutional discipline and high-impact corporate transactions.
